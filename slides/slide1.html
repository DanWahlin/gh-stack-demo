THE PROBLEM

A large feature creates an awkward choice

Optimize for review speed, or development speed?

OPTION 1

One giant PR

Everything arrives at once. Reviewers must untangle the model, validation, and tests together.

OPTION 2

Small, serial PRs

The diffs are focused, but dependent work waits for every previous PR to merge.

Giant PR: slow review   •   Serial PRs: stop-and-wait development
